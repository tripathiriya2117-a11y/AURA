AURA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. About AURA

AURA is a personal AI assistant developed for private, personal use.

2. Information We Access

When AURA is connected to a third-party service, it may access information that the user explicitly authorizes through that service's authentication system.

For Pinterest, this may include boards, Pins, and related account information permitted by the API permissions granted to AURA.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected services is used solely to provide AURA's functionality, including helping the user organize, search, and interact with their information.

4. Data Sharing

AURA does not sell personal information or share connected service data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage

Authentication credentials and connected-service data are stored only as necessary for AURA's functionality and are intended for use by the authorized user.

6. Third-Party Services

AURA may connect to third-party services such as Pinterest. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, terms, and data practices.

7. Data Deletion

The user may disconnect a connected service and request deletion of data associated with that service.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the AURA developer through the contact information associated with the AURA project.
